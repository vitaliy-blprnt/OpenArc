Before running

The probe creates a separate test window, synthetic bookmarks, and one page on https://example.com. It leaves a local report and may leave synthetic recently closed/history entries. It never clears browsing history.

Leave the test window and bookmarks untouched while checks run. The session check inspects only the newest recently closed entry, compares its URL with this run's unique marker, and restores nothing if they differ. Unrelated entry data is neither recorded nor displayed.
